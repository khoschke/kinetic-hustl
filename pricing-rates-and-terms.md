## How to use this file

This is the **price list** and the **rates of record**. Everything here is safe to quote to a client, and this is the file to use whenever pricing needs to be communicated — an enquiry, a change of arrangement, a rate update, an invoice question.

**When writing anything a client will read:**

- Quote rates only from the tables below. Do not calculate, estimate, or infer a rate that is not written here.
- If a client's situation is not covered — an unusual frequency, a discount request, a legacy rate, a format not listed — say so and ask Karl. Do not invent a number.
- Do not discuss what a session earns, revenue per slot, margins, how rates are constructed, or how they compare to competitors. None of that belongs in client-facing writing, and none of it is in this file.
- Do not describe any option as a worse deal, inefficient, or a way of encouraging a client toward something else. Present the options plainly and let the client choose.
- Prices include GST. Say so.

## Personal training

### 1-on-1

All rates below are **per session**.

| Frequency | With app access | Without app access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Weekly | $88 per session | $110 per session |
| Flexi (fortnightly) | $104.50 per session | $126.50 per session |
| Casual | $121 per session | $143 per session |

**Weekly** means one session a week or more — there is no upper limit, and every session is charged at the same per-session rate. **Flexi** means a minimum of one session per fortnight, billed fortnightly.

A client's tier is set by their agreement, not by how many sessions happen to fall in a given fortnight. Additional or rescheduled sessions are charged at the client's agreed rate and do not change it. Moving between weekly and flexi happens by agreement, going forward, when a client's ongoing pattern changes — a new agreement is issued at that point.

**Casual** rates apply differently: $121 is for an existing client who holds app access; $143 is for anyone who is not a regular client, online or in person.

## App access and online coaching

| Item | Price |
| --- | --- |
| App access, alongside weekly or flexi training | $22 per week |
| Online coaching — programming and app access, no session commitment | $44 per week |

App access includes personalised programming, progress tracking, and message support through the Kinetic Hustl app.

App access is $22 per week however often you train, while the session discount applies to each session. The two cancel out at one session a week, so app access is free at the margin there. Above one session a week it saves you money; below one session a week it adds to the cost.

What that works out to:

| Your training | With app access | Without app access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 session per week | $110 per week | $110 per week |
| 2 sessions per week | $198 per week | $220 per week |
| 1 session per fortnight (flexi) | $148.50 per fortnight | $126.50 per fortnight |

Quote the totals in that table, not the session rate alone, whenever a client is comparing the two options. A flexi client reading only the session rates will assume app access saves them $22 when in fact it adds $22, and that is exactly the kind of surprise that has caused confusion before.

Additional sessions are added at your agreed session rate — $88 for weekly clients, $104.50 for flexi clients. A weekly client adding a second, third or fourth session pays $88 for each.

## Terms

- **Minimum term:** 12 weeks, unless agreed otherwise in writing.
- **Session cancellation and credits:** 24 hours. Sessions cancelled with 24 hours' notice or more are credited to your account, and a credit can be used to rebook straight away or held and used later. Sessions cancelled inside 24 hours are forfeited.
- **Credit expiry:** credits expire three months from the date of the cancelled session. Karl may extend a credit at his discretion. Credits stay usable through your notice period and lapse when the agreement ends. They are not refundable and cannot be transferred to another person.
- **Direct debit cancellation:** 30 days' notice, by email.
- **Payment:** direct debit through Ezidebit on your training cycle — weekly for weekly clients, fortnightly for flexi clients. App access is $22 per week, so a flexi fortnight is one session at $104.50 plus two weeks of app access at $44, totalling $148.50. Direct deposit can be arranged.
- **Transaction fees:** card payments are debited at the agreed amount, with no surcharge. If you pay by bank account direct debit, Ezidebit charges $0.99 per debit, added to the agreed amount and shown on the debit. That fee is Ezidebit's, not Kinetic Hustl's, and can change.
- **Travel and time away:** if you would like to keep training while you are away, your programming can be adapted to whatever equipment and facilities you have access to. If you would rather not train, your sessions can be rescheduled.
- **Trial:** a complimentary first session is often available.
- **Junior clients (under 18):** standard rates apply, with no junior rate. A parent or guardian signs the rate agreement and a Junior Client Addendum, and the junior signs their own Participation Agreement. Minimum age is 12. For clients **under 16**, a responsible adult must be present for the whole session, and written practitioner clearance is required before loaded training where there is a surgical history, a diagnosed condition or an ongoing treating practitioner. No progress photos are taken of anyone under 18.

### Shared sessions (2-on-1 and 3-on-1)

- Each participant has their own agreement and their own payment arrangement, and is not responsible for anyone else's fees.
- If another participant cannot attend, your rate does not change. The session runs as scheduled for whoever attends.
- If a participant leaves permanently, you continue at your agreed rate for two weeks while a replacement is found. Karl will then confirm in writing the rate for the new group size. Your rate changes only if you agree in writing, and you may end the agreement at that point without further notice or fee.

## Presenting these rates

When a client asks for pricing, lead with the format they asked about, showing both rates. Then app access at $22 per week, noting it costs nothing extra at one session a week. Then online coaching for anyone who wants programming without a session commitment.

Keep app access as a single line item — it is one thing, and splitting programming from app access reads as two charges.

If asked why the with-app session rate is lower: the app fee replaces the difference. At one session a week it costs the same either way, and beyond that it works out cheaper.
