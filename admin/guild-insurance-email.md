# Email to Guild Insurance — cover for clients under 16

**Status:** not yet sent
**Policy:** P00329386 · Client A3938736 · renewed 10/10/2026 to 10/10/2027

## How to send it

**The policy documents contain no email address.** The only contact details across the schedule, the
certificates and the 38-page PDS are the phone number **1800 810 213** and the portal at
**guildinsurance.com.au/policyhub**.

So either:

1. **Send it through PolicyHub's enquiry or message function.** Better than email for this purpose: it is
   logged against the policy, so the questions and the answers sit with the account rather than in an
   inbox.
2. **Or call 1800 810 213, say you need written answers to five underwriting questions, and ask them to
   email you** so you can reply with the text below. That also gets you a named person to chase.

Do not settle for the answers over the phone. The point of asking is to have them on file.

## Why this is being asked

The policy carries a general exclusion at page 25 of the PDS:

> **Working with Children** — any persons under 16 years old participating in exercise programs or
> classes conducted by You.

The only carve-back is an additional benefit at page 14, inside the **Professional Indemnity** section,
conditional on six things. The **Public and Products Liability** section (pages 17 to 18) contains no
additional benefits at all, so on a plain reading a bodily injury claim from a client under 16 is
excluded with nothing restoring it. That is the claim most likely to actually happen.

Karl's current youngest client is 12. Youngest ever, 10.

Answers to questions 1 to 4 decide how the junior clauses in the client agreement are written. Question 3
in particular: the agreement currently requires a responsible adult **other than Karl** to be present for
every under-16 session, which is the strict reading. If Karl's own supervision satisfies the condition,
that clause relaxes considerably.

## The email

**Subject:** Policy P00329386 — written confirmation on cover for clients under 16

---

Hi,

I hold Fitness Active Lifestyle Professionals Liability Insurance, policy P00329386, renewed for
10/10/2026 to 10/10/2027. I train clients under 16 and I need a few things confirmed in writing before I
continue.

I have read the "Working with Children" exclusion and the "Child Participation in Classes and Programs"
additional benefit in the Professional Indemnity section. My questions are:

**1. Which sections respond to a claim involving a client under 16?** The child participation benefit
appears under Professional Indemnity. The Public and Products Liability section does not appear to
contain an equivalent provision. If a client under 16 suffered a bodily injury during a session and
brought a claim, would the Public Liability limit respond, and if so under what provision?

**2. What qualifies as "relevant qualifications and training to qualify as a children's trainer"?** I
hold a Bachelor of Exercise and Movement Science. Does that satisfy condition i, and if not, which
specific qualifications or courses do you accept?

**3. Condition vi requires that children are "under adult supervision at all times".** I am the
supervising adult during every session and I hold a current Queensland Blue Card. Does my supervision
satisfy this condition, or does it require a parent, guardian or other adult present in addition to me?

**4. What do you require as an "asthma management plan including an emergency response plan"?** I collect
asthma history, reliever medication, triggers and emergency contacts at intake. Is a written plan per
client required, and is there a format you accept?

**5. Separately, the "Specific Activities" exclusion excludes bodily injury connected with "body
building, body re-composition, power or olympic lifting or lifting for the purposes of sporting
competition".** I do not offer bodybuilding, powerlifting, Olympic lifting or competition preparation as
services, and none of my clients compete or perform in those disciplines. My clients train for general
health, strength and body composition goals using resistance training methods that are influenced by
those disciplines. Can you confirm where you draw the line, and that ordinary resistance training for a
non-competing client is not caught by this exclusion?

I also want to formally note that I train clients under 16, so this is recorded against my policy for
disclosure purposes. My current youngest client is 12.

I would appreciate written responses rather than a phone call, as I need these on file.

Kind regards,
Karl Hoschke
Kinetic Hustl · ABN 51 607 358 310
Policy P00329386 · Client A3938736

---

## When the reply comes back

- **Answer 3 is the one to act on first.** It decides clause 8 of the junior section in the T&C form.
- File the reply with the policy documents. It is the evidence that the six conditions were checked
  rather than assumed.
- If they name a children's trainer qualification you do not hold, that becomes a blocker for under-16
  clients until it is done.
- Record the outcome in `terms/terms-and-conditions.md` under Decisions, the way the Ezidebit
  confirmation was recorded.
