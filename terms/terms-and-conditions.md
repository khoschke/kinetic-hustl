# Kinetic Hustl — Terms and Conditions (master source)

**Version:** October 2026 (rev 2)
**Status:** Source of truth. The ten client-facing `.docx` templates in this folder are generated from it.

Rates below follow `pricing-rates-and-terms.md`. If a rate changes there, it changes here, and the templates are regenerated.

---

## One file for Claude projects

`client-agreements-complete.md` carries the full text of all ten agreements — every clause, every rate,
every variation — in a single file to add to a project. It is generated by the same script as the `.docx`
templates from the same clause constants, so it cannot drift from what clients actually sign. Do not edit
it by hand; change the script and re-run.

---

## Collecting agreement by form

`google-form-questions.md` holds the question set for the Google Form clients complete instead of
initialling a printed document. Every acknowledgement is a required question, so the form cannot be
submitted without it — which is the actual fix for policies being skipped, rather than document
formatting. Build it as one form with branching from the first question, not ten separate forms.

---

## Regenerating the templates

The ten `.docx` files are generated, not hand-edited. Edit this file for wording, then
`terms/generate-templates.js` for what goes into the documents, and run:

```
npm install docx          # once
node terms/generate-templates.js
```

It writes the ten `KH-Terms-*.docx` files and `client-agreements-complete.md` into `terms/`, alongside the script, no matter which directory you run it from. Editing a `.docx`
by hand works but the change is lost the next time anything is regenerated, so put wording
changes in the script.

The price sheet works the same way: `pricesheet.html` is the source, and the PDF is
produced by printing it to A4 from a browser.

## Template set

Ten templates. Weekly and flexi are separate documents (they are the only formats with a frequency tier),
and the four in-person formats are each issued in a with-app-access and a without-app-access version.

**Online Coaching and Casual have one version each.** Online coaching *is* app access, so a without-app
version would be nothing; casual clients who hold app access are online coaching clients, so a
casual-with-app version would duplicate it. This matches the branch structure of the client form. Each document states one set of rates and speaks only to that client's situation, rather than presenting both columns and asking the client to work out which applies to them.

Each document names one billing cycle rather than offering "week / fortnight" as a blank to fill: the weekly documents say weekly direct debit and *my weekly payment amount*, the flexi documents say fortnightly and note that each debit carries two weeks of app access. The casual documents drop the minimum-term line entirely, since none applies.

The with/without split also changes the **Your information** clause: the with-app versions say data is held by the third-party app provider as well as by Karl, the without-app versions say it is held in Karl's records only.

**Every participant in a shared session signs their own copy.** Never one agreement covering two people — it fails the moment one of them leaves.

## Section 3 — App access and online coaching

- Access to the Kinetic Hustl App is **$22 per week (GST incl.)** and is optional. It is available to clients training weekly or fortnightly.
- Clients without a session commitment may take **online coaching at $44 per week (GST incl.)**, which includes app access and programming.
- Session rates differ depending on whether app access is held. Both rates are shown in the agreement schedule.

## Section 4 — Fees

- All prices include GST.
- Payment is by direct debit through Ezidebit **on the client's training cycle** — weekly for weekly clients, fortnightly for flexi clients — unless otherwise agreed. App access is $22 per week, so a flexi fortnight includes two weeks of it ($104.50 + $44 = $148.50). Direct deposit can be arranged.
- **Card payments carry no surcharge from 1 October 2026.** This applies to all cards, domestic and international. The card fee is deducted from Kinetic Hustl's settlement rather than added to the client's debit.
- **Bank account direct debit fees are unchanged.** Any Ezidebit bank account fee is charged in addition to the amounts below and appears on the debit.
- A minimum term of 12 weeks applies unless otherwise agreed in writing.

**Weekly and flexi (1-on-1 only).** Weekly means one session per week or more. There is no upper limit and the same per-session rate applies to every session. Flexi means a minimum of one session per fortnight, billed fortnightly. A client's tier is set by their agreement, not by how many sessions fall in a given fortnight. Additional or rescheduled sessions are charged at the client's agreed rate and do not change it. Moving between weekly and flexi happens by agreement, going forward.

## Section 4a — Your information

Covers what is collected (personal details, health and medical information, training records, body composition), what it is used for, where it is stored including the third-party app provider, that it is never sold, the two circumstances in which it is shared (at the client's request, or where required by law), and the client's right to access, correct or delete it.

## Section 4b — Photos and video

Progress photos are for the client's own tracking and are never shared or published **without consent** — worded that way deliberately so that a future opportunity to celebrate a client's progress is not ruled out by the terms. Marketing and social media use is optional and consented to by ticking a box, which also covers progress photos. Consent is withdrawable at any time.

## Before you send one: review checklist

Work through this once. Most items are quick; two need a decision and one needs outside advice.

### Check first

- [ ] **Open each `.docx` in Word.** These were generated in a sandbox where LibreOffice could not render, so the layout has not been seen — only verified structurally. Check table widths, page breaks, and that the ☐ checkbox glyph renders rather than showing as a box or question mark.
- [ ] **Confirm the consent wording is your current version.** It is reproduced verbatim from `Kinetic Hustl. Terms and Conditions KH App.docx` (July 2026), the most recent of several variants on file. If a different one is authoritative, say so and the templates get regenerated.
- [ ] **Read the attendance clause as a client would.** It is the one clause that reverses previous practice, and the first shared-format client to lose a partner for a week will read it closely.
- [ ] **Decide whether to pre-fill the 12-week minimum** in the closing block, or leave it blank per client.

### Decisions

- [x] ~~Gym access not mentioned~~ — added as an initialled policy row in all ten templates.
- [x] ~~No photo/media consent, no privacy clause~~ — both added. See the flag below on the privacy one.
- [x] ~~No business identification block~~ — added under the title. ABN 51 607 358 310 is in all ten templates.
- [x] ~~One-sided signature block~~ — a trainer counter-signature line now sits under the client's.
- [x] ~~Card surcharge and bank account fees~~ — Card surcharge removed from 1 October 2026 for all cards, domestic and international. Bank account fees unchanged and still added to bank account debits (confirmed by Ezidebit, Sep 2026).
- [ ] **Confirm whether the Privacy Act applies to you.** Small businesses under $3M turnover are generally exempt, but that exemption does not extend to businesses providing a health service and holding health information — which a personal trainer collecting medical history and body composition may well be. If it applies, the clause added here is a starting point rather than a complete compliance position, and you would also need a privacy policy. Worth asking whoever reviews the 12-week term to look at this at the same time. Not legal advice.

### Get advice on this one

- [ ] **The 12-week minimum term.** Minimum-term clauses in standard-form consumer contracts can attract scrutiny under the Australian Consumer Law unfair-contract-terms regime, where penalties have applied since November 2023. It is common practice in the industry and you have used it for years, so this is a "have it looked at once" item rather than a red flag.

  The group-size clause previously sat here too, because it varied a client's price automatically. It has since been rewritten to be consent-based — the rate changes only if the client agrees in writing, and they can exit at that point without notice or fee — which removes the unilateral element.

  I am not a lawyer and none of this is legal advice.
