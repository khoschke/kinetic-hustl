# Junior onboarding flow

**Kinetic Hustl — Karl Hoschke**
**Current as at:** 12 November 2026
**Applies to:** every client under 18

Internal reference. It assembles steps that live in other documents rather than restating them.
Where this disagrees with the T&C form, the Guardian Addendum or the asthma procedure, those win
and this gets fixed.

---

## 1. Enquiry and booking

- Guardian completes the **health screening form**, including the asthma block in section 4 and,
  where asthma is indicated, section 5.
- Guardian completes the **T&C form**, which includes the junior terms in section 4: medical
  clearance, asthma, supervision, drop-off, photos, messaging.
- Two-way messaging is off unless the guardian turns it on at question 13 of that section.

## 2. Before the first session

Nothing is booked in until these are on file.

- **Practitioner clearance**, where there is a surgical history, a diagnosed condition or an
  ongoing treating practitioner. No loaded training before it arrives.
- **Asthma Action Plan**, where asthma is indicated. Signed by their doctor, current, triggers
  listed. **No plan, no first session.** Save the master procedure link in the client's record
  next to the plan.
- A copy of the plan goes in the session kit as well as the client file.

> The health screening form offers a file upload for the plan, but it is not the gate and is not
> marked Required: Google Forms uploads only work for respondents signed into a Google account.
> The gate is this step and clause 4 of the Guardian Addendum.

## 3. First session

- Five minutes with the guardian before starting: walk through the Action Plan, check the reliever
  and spacer are present and in date, check the athlete's technique with the device.
- Point the guardian to the **parent guide** so they have it in writing.
- Where the athlete has a Participation Agreement to sign, do it here, in person. If messaging has
  been turned on, that includes the messaging item, and the athlete needs to understand their
  guardian can read the thread before the channel goes live.
- Turn two-way messaging on only once both the guardian's choice and the athlete's acknowledgement
  exist.

## 4. Every session

- Run the **pre-session checklist** from the master procedure. Two minutes.
- Any "no" in the non-negotiables block means no session that day.
- Symptoms in the last 24 hours, or reliever used more than usual: light session at most, and tell
  the guardian. Thunderstorm asthma warning for Brisbane: reschedule.

## 5. Ongoing

- Log the Action Plan's **signed date** and **next review date** in the client record, with a
  reminder.
- Re-check at every 12-week block, and whenever medication changes.
- A new plan from the doctor replaces the old one on file, in the kit as well as the record.
- Under-16s get the full health screen annually, not the short one.
