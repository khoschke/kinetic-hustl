Terms

  • Minimum term — 12 weeks, unless agreed otherwise.
  • Session cancellation — 24 hours. Cancel with 24 hours' notice or more and the session is credited, to rebook now or later; inside 24 hours it is forfeited. Credits last three months.
  • Direct debit cancellation — 30 days' notice by email.
  • Payment — direct debit via Ezidebit on your training cycle: weekly for weekly clients, fortnightly for flexi. Direct deposit can be arranged. Card payments carry no surcharge; any Ezidebit bank account fee applies to bank account debits.
  • Travel — if you'd like to keep training while away, your programming can be adapted to whatever equipment you have access to. If you'd rather not train, your sessions can be rescheduled.
  • Shared sessions — each person has their own agreement. If someone can't attend, your rate doesn't change.
